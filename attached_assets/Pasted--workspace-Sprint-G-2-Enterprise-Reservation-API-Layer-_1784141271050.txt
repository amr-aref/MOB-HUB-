# BUSINESS RULES

Implement proper reservation rules.

Examples:

A product cannot have multiple confirmed reservations.

Merchant confirms only one reservation.

Pending reservations remain pending until decision.

Confirmation automatically updates remaining reservations according to the approved business rules.

Cancelled reservations cannot return to pending.

Completed reservations are immutable.

Prevent invalid transitions.

Prevent duplicate reservations.

Prevent inconsistent data.

Implement the rules inside the Service Layer.

Never inside controllers.
