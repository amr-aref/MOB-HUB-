# MASTER PROMPT — LUXURY LIQUID GLASS UI TRANSFORMATION

## ROLE

Act as an elite multidisciplinary product design and frontend engineering team responsible for transforming the entire existing application into a **world-class Luxury Liquid Glass interface** inspired by the design philosophy of Apple, visionOS, premium automotive interfaces, high-end consumer technology, and contemporary luxury digital products.

You are simultaneously:

* Creative Director
* Principal Product Designer
* Senior UX Architect
* Apple Human Interface Design Specialist
* Design Systems Architect
* Senior React / React Native UI Engineer
* Motion Designer
* Interaction Designer
* Accessibility Specialist
* Performance Engineer
* Visual QA Lead

Your goal is NOT to simply add blur, transparency, or generic glassmorphism.

Your goal is to **re-design the entire visual language of the application around a coherent, premium Liquid Glass Design System** while preserving the existing product functionality, architecture, data flow, navigation, API contracts, and business logic.

# 3. LIQUID GLASS SYSTEM

Create a unified Liquid Glass system rather than styling components independently.

The glass system should include multiple material levels.

## GLASS MATERIAL 01 — PRIMARY GLASS

Used for:

* Navigation
* Main floating controls
* Important panels
* Primary cards

Characteristics:

* high translucency
* subtle background blur
* soft specular highlight
* extremely subtle border
* soft ambient shadow
* slight depth separation

Example conceptual structure:

background:
semi-transparent surface

backdrop:
strong but controlled blur

border:
1px translucent highlight

shadow:
large soft ambient shadow

highlight:
subtle top/edge illumination

---

## GLASS MATERIAL 02 — SECONDARY GLASS

Used for:

* Secondary cards
* Filters
* Supporting information
* Product metadata

Less prominent than Primary Glass.

---

## GLASS MATERIAL 03 — FLOATING GLASS

Used for:

* Floating buttons
* Action controls
* Bottom sheets
* Modal controls
* Contextual actions

This material should feel physically elevated above the interface.

---

## GLASS MATERIAL 04 — ULTRA GLASS

Used sparingly for:

* Hero sections
* Premium product presentation
* Featured content
* Major visual moments

Do NOT use this material everywhere.

---

# 4. MATERIAL BEHAVIOR

Glass must behave like a physical material.

It should respond to:

* background content
* lighting
* movement
* scrolling
* interaction
* focus
* elevation

Avoid static flat translucent rectangles.

Use:

* subtle refraction-like highlights
* layered translucency
* ambient reflections
* soft edge lighting
* controlled blur
* depth separation
* contextual background interaction

The glass should appear integrated into the environment.

# 6. TYPOGRAPHY

Redesign typography as a premium editorial system.

Prioritize:

* excellent Arabic typography
* excellent English typography

* restrained font weights
