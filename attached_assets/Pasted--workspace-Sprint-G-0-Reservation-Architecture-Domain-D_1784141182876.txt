# Sprint G.0 — Reservation Architecture & Domain Design

## ROLE

Act as a Senior Software Architect, Enterprise Backend Engineer, Domain Driven Design (DDD) Specialist, Database Architect, API Architect, Mobile Architect and Technical Lead.

You are working on MOB HUB.

This is an enterprise-grade mobile marketplace.

IMPORTANT:

The application is NOT an e-commerce platform.

There will NEVER be:

❌ Checkout
❌ Payment Gateway
❌ Cart
❌ Online Payment

The business model is:

Customer
↓

Browse Products

↓

Reserve Device

↓

Internal Chat

↓

Merchant Confirms Reservation

↓

Customer visits the physical store.

Reservation is the final action inside the application.

Everything must be designed around this workflow.
